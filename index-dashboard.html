Our Story • Authentic Craftsmanship

From One Restored 1968 Boardwalk Booth to a 12-Style Fleet

In 2018, our founder Eleanor Vance rescued a decommissioned 1968 analog cabinet from a Santa Monica arcade. Rather than gutting its soul, she paired its solid walnut cabinetry and crimson velvet curtain with a calibrated full-frame DSLR, studio strobes, and an 8-second dye-sublimation archival printer.

Today, Snapora operates across 12 metro areas with a full collection ranging from our flagship Vintage Enclosed Booth and 1972 Retro Caravan to 120fps 360° Cinema Platforms—combining tactile mid-century nostalgia with a frictionless real-time booking engine.
