Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How early does your team arrive for setup, and is setup time included?

Every rental includes complimentary arrival and setup 60 to 90 minutes prior to your scheduled booth start time. Setup and breakdown never eat into your paid active booth hours!

Can you provide a Certificate of Insurance (COI) for our wedding or corporate venue?

Yes! We carry $2,000,000 in commercial general liability insurance and happily issue custom-named Certificates of Insurance (COI) to your venue management at no extra charge.

What if our venue does not have reliable Wi-Fi for SMS and QR sharing?

Every Snapora booth comes equipped with its own multi-carrier 5G cellular hotspot, and our local AirDrop/QR server works even in subterranean ballrooms or remote vineyards.

How many prints do guests receive per session?

On our unlimited print packages, if 6 friends jump into the photo together, our attendant prints 6 copies on the spot—plus an extra copy mounted straight into your keepsake guestbook!
