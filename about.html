Our Brand Story

From One Restored 1968 Boardwalk Booth to a 12-Style Fleet

In 2018, our founder Eleanor Vance rescued a decommissioned 1968 analog cabinet from a Santa Monica arcade. Rather than gutting its soul, she paired its solid walnut cabinetry and crimson velvet curtain with a calibrated full-frame DSLR, parabolic studio strobes, and an 8-second sub-dye archival printer.

The response at our very first vineyard wedding was electric. Guests lined up all night—grandparents, flower girls, and college friends alike—captivated by the warmth of real optical glass and thick matte paper. Today, Snapora operates across 12 metro areas with a full collection ranging from our flagship Vintage Enclosed Booth and 1972 Retro Caravan ("Clementine") to 120fps 360° Cinema Platforms.

"Technology should never feel cold at a celebration. Whether it is a mahogany booth or a 360° video rig, our job is to make every guest feel like the star of an editorial cover shoot."

— Eleanor Vance, Founder & Creative Director
